# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

- First release: open jobs from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday career boards in one schema, with title, location, country, remote/hybrid/onsite, employment type, salary range when published, posted date and apply link. Filters for title keywords, excluded words, location, remote only and posting age; optional plain-text description; auto-detection of the ATS from a bare company name.
